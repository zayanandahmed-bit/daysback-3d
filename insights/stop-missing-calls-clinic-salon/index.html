How to Stop Missing Calls at Your Clinic, Salon or Practice

22 October 2026 · 5 min read · DaysBack

In appointment-based businesses the phone rings at the worst moments: when the receptionist is checking someone in, taking a payment or on another line, and before opening or after closing. Every call that goes unanswered is someone who may book with the next business on their list.

Step 1: Find out how many you miss

Most phone systems can show a log of missed calls. Look at a normal week: how many were missed, at what times, and how many of those numbers were new people rather than existing customers. The pattern usually points straight at the fix.

Step 2: Fix the quick wins

  • Text back every missed call automatically with a booking link
  • Put online booking on your website, Google profile and Instagram
  • Make sure your voicemail says when you will call back, and actually do

Step 3: Let an AI receptionist answer the overflow

An AI receptionist picks up when the front desk cannot, and after hours. It answers in a natural voice, handles common questions like prices, parking and opening times, books straight into your diary, and takes a message for anything it should not handle. Your team stays in charge; the AI only covers the calls that would otherwise ring out.

What to set up carefully

  • Clear rules for urgent or clinical calls, so they always reach a person or the right service
  • A connection to your real booking system, so it never double-books
  • Your tone and your policies, so it sounds like your business
  • A short summary of every call, sent to the team

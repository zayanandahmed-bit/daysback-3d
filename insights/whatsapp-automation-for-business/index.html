WhatsApp Automation for Business: What You Can Automate

22 October 2026 · 5 min read · DaysBack

For many customers WhatsApp is the easiest way to reach a business. The catch is that every message needs a human, which doesn't scale. With the WhatsApp Business Platform you can automate the repetitive parts and keep the personal touch for the conversations that need it.

What you can automate on WhatsApp

  • Instant replies to new messages, any time of day
  • An AI assistant that answers questions about your services and prices
  • Appointment booking, confirmations and reminders
  • Follow-ups for quotes and enquiries that went quiet
  • Order updates, delivery tracking and receipts
  • Review requests after a job is done

Keeping it personal

Good automation does not feel like a robot. Messages use the customer's name and context, the AI hands over to a person when the question needs one, and your team can step into any conversation at any time.

Rules worth knowing

WhatsApp has clear rules: customers need to have opted in, and messages you send first (outside a 24-hour reply window) use pre-approved templates. Following them keeps your number in good standing, and we set this up properly from day one.

Is it worth it?

If a meaningful share of your enquiries already come in on WhatsApp, almost certainly. It is one of the fastest ways to reply to leads in seconds and stop customers slipping through. Book a free 27-minute strategy call and we will map out what your WhatsApp could handle.
